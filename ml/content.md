## What This Section Covers

This page is for machine learning notes written with scientific datasets in mind, especially where data is limited and physical interpretation matters.

## Current Tutorial Direction

### Linear Regression for Scientific Data
A first tutorial on building intuition for linear regression, fitting a simple model, interpreting coefficients, and thinking clearly about residuals and error metrics.

### Feature Engineering and Descriptors
How to build meaningful inputs for materials-related datasets and why descriptor quality often matters more than model complexity.

### Evaluation in Low-Data Regimes
Notes on train-test splits, cross-validation, overfitting, and realistic expectations when the dataset is small.
