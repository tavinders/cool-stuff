## What This Section Covers

This page will collect tutorial notes on the physical ideas behind density functional theory, along with practical guidance for setting up and checking calculations.

## Suggested Tutorial Tracks

### SCF and Convergence
How self-consistent field cycles work, why convergence matters, and how to recognize when numerical settings are hiding physical mistakes.

### k-Points and Cutoff Testing
A practical guide to testing sampling and basis settings so the final results are stable enough to trust.

### Band Structures and DOS
How to read electronic structure outputs and connect them back to a materials question rather than treating plots as isolated outputs.

## How Notes Will Be Written

Each tutorial can be written as a compact markdown note with explanations, equations, screenshots, and short implementation checklists.

## Downloadable Versions

When a note is polished enough, it can also be attached here as a downloadable PDF for offline reading.
