About Me

Working where physics, materials simulations, and machine learning meet.

Tavinder Singh

Theoretical Physics VII

University of Bayreuth, Germany

My research focuses on bridging first-principles physics and machine learning to better understand and design energy materials. Using density functional theory (DFT), I study phenomena such as polaron formation and transport, as well as ion transport in battery materials, while increasingly exploring applications in solar cells.

A key part of my work involves developing data-efficient approaches that learn from small but information-rich datasets. I am particularly interested in combining physically meaningful descriptors with machine learning methods and generating new candidate structures using modern interatomic potentials.

My broader goal is to create approaches that are not only accurate but also interpretable, reproducible, and genuinely useful for real scientific workflows.
